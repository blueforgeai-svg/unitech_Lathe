Our Story

From a single bay to a 78-model catalog.

Uni-Tech Machines was founded in 1986 by Mr. Kiranbhai Vachhani — a single-bay workshop on Gondal Road, Rajkot, turning rollers for a local mill. Forty years on, that bay has grown into a full production plant covering lathes, hydraulic presses, drills, slotters, shapers, bandsaws and milling machines.

We are a sole proprietorship with 26–50 skilled engineers and technicians on the floor. Every machine that leaves our gate is bolted, aligned, run-in and signed off by a named engineer — no shortcuts, no mystery.

How We Build

Three principles, forty years.

Bolted, aligned, run-in

Every machine is fully assembled, dial-aligned, broken-in and signed off by a named engineer before it leaves the works.

Cast iron, seasoned hard

Beds are flame-hardened and castings seasoned for stability before machining — so the machine holds its line under load for decades.

A name on every tag

Behind every machine is an engineer who is accountable for it — even ten years later. That's how a workshop runs for forty years.

Milestones

Forty years on Plot 04.

1986
Workshop founded by Kiranbhai Vachhani
A single bay on Gondal Road, light-duty lathe contracts for Rajkot mills — one cone-pulley lathe and three apprentices.
1992
Heavy-duty series launched
First 7-foot heavy-duty lathe ships to a steel rolling mill in Bhavnagar. The series goes on to become a workshop staple.
1998
First 20-foot plano-type bed
A sugar corporation orders a 20-foot plano-type lathe for roller turning. The bay had to be extended to fit the machine.
2009
ISO 9001:2008 certification
Quality management system rolled out across every model — named engineers, signed off, with traceable batch records.
2018
First export — Nigeria
Heavy-duty lathes shipped to the West-African oil sector. The export desk has since opened twelve markets.
2026
78 production models
A complete range across seven categories — light-duty bench lathes through 20-foot plano-type, plus hydraulic press, slotting, drilling, shaping, bandsaw and milling.
